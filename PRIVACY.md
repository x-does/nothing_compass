# Glyph North privacy policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026.

Glyph North is an independent clock and compass app for compatible Nothing Glyph Matrix phones. Project support is available at [github.com/x-does/nothing_compass/issues](https://github.com/x-does/nothing_compass/issues).

## Data used on your phone

The app processes accelerometer and compass-related sensor readings on the device to detect orientation, shakes and magnetic north. It does not upload sensor data or save a sensor history. It does not request location access.

Display preferences and connection diagnostics are stored in private app storage. A copied diagnostic report can include your phone model, Android/build details, sensor/output timing and error messages. Android cloud backup and device transfer are excluded. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data.

## Collection and sharing

Glyph North has no internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics or automatic developer-operated data collection. Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to copy and share connection details, you control the destination; review the report before posting it. Public GitHub issues are visible to other people.

## Permissions and background use

- The Nothing Glyph permission communicates with the device's Glyph service.
- Notifications show the active live session and a Stop control.
- Foreground-service and special-use permissions support the explicitly started rear clock/compass while the front screen is off.
- Motion sensors and a CPU wake lock support the active session and use battery. Stop from the app, tile or notification when finished.

The app does not request Accessibility, Shizuku, camera, microphone, contacts or SMS access. Essential Key remapping is not included.

## Device software

The app uses Nothing's proprietary Glyph Matrix SDK. Nothing OS, Android and the services through which you download or share the app have their own privacy practices, independently of this app.

## Contact and changes

Ask privacy questions through the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/x-does/nothing_compass/issues), without posting sensitive information. This policy will be updated when app behavior changes. Publisher contact details for a future Google Play release will also be supplied in that listing.
