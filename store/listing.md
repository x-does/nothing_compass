# Glyph North

## Short description

A clock and shake-to-compass experience for your Nothing Glyph Matrix.

## Full description

A little time. A little direction.

Glyph North turns the rear Glyph Matrix on compatible Nothing phones into a compact clock and magnetic compass.

START WITH TIME
See a clean 24-hour clock, with hours above minutes.

SHAKE FOR NORTH
Hold the phone screen down, with the Glyph facing up, and give it a deliberate shake to switch to the compass. Flip the screen face up to return to the clock.

ONE SHORTCUT, THREE MODES
Add the Quick Settings tile to cycle through Off, Time and Compass. Stop the live session from the app or its notification.

MAKE IT COMFORTABLE
Adjust brightness and rear display orientation. Get clear calibration guidance and connection help when you need it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, analytics, internet connection or location permission. Sensor processing happens on your phone.

COMPATIBILITY
Rear display control requires Nothing Phone (4a) Pro or Nothing Phone (3), compatible Nothing OS firmware and Glyph Interface enabled. Other phones can explore the on-screen preview. System Glyph activity may temporarily take display priority. Deselect Glyph North as the system Always-on Glyph Toy when using live mode.

The compass points to magnetic north, not geographic true north. Hold the phone level and away from magnets for the best result. Live display and motion sensing use battery while active; end the session when finished.

Glyph North is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nothing Technology Limited.
