# Glyph North privacy policy

Draft effective date: 4 November 2026. Publisher contact must be supplied before publication.

Glyph North displays a clock and magnetic compass on supported Nothing Glyph Matrix phones.

## Data and sensor processing

The app processes accelerometer and compass-related sensor readings on the device to identify orientation, shakes and magnetic north. Sensor readings are not transmitted or retained as a history. The app does not request location access or calculate geographic true north from your location.

Display preferences, permission-prompt state and connection diagnostics are kept in private app storage. Diagnostics may include device/build information, sensor/output timing and error messages. Android backup and device transfer are excluded. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local app data.

## Collection and sharing

The app has no internet permission, analytics, advertising, accounts or developer-operated data collection. It does not automatically send diagnostics. If you choose Copy details, the report is placed on your clipboard for you to share; review it before sharing and consider the privacy practices of the destination you choose.

## Permissions and background operation

- Nothing Glyph permission connects to the phone's Glyph system service.
- Notifications provide information and a Stop control for a live session.
- Foreground-service and special-use permissions support the user-started clock/compass experience with the front screen off.
- A CPU wake lock and active motion sensors support that live session. Stop from the app, Quick Settings or notification to end live use.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, Accessibility or Shizuku access. Essential Key remapping is not part of this release.

## Third-party component

The app integrates Nothing's proprietary Glyph Matrix SDK to communicate with the device's system service. Nothing OS and your Android platform may process their own system information independently of this app.
